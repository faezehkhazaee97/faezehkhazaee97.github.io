About

Click the photo

Faezeh (Baran) Khazaee is an M.Sc. student in Computer Science at the University of California, Davis (expected December 2026), where she works in the Quon Lab on machine learning and computational biology.

Her core strength is deep learning: transformers, self-supervised and representation learning, and generative modeling. She applies these to large-scale single-cell and genomic data, and earlier applied them to computer vision and multimodal reasoning. She cares about models that are interpretable, so a prediction about a patient or a gene can be traced to the cells and sequences that drove it.

Before UC Davis, she studied information technology engineering at the University of Tehran and Sharif University of Technology, and built machine learning systems as a freelance data scientist.

Research

My projects share one question: what can a deep model learn about biology from raw data, and can we see how it learned it?

1 / 4
Single cells

CELLECTION

Interpretable multiple-instance learning that predicts a donor’s phenotype from thousands of single cells and points to the cells behind it. I led benchmarking against eight baselines on five tasks, validation, figures, and the manuscript.

Benchmark code

Gene regulation

ProMod and P2G

ProMod is a self-supervised transformer trained on promoter DNA from more than 100 vertebrates. P2G adds chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq) and histone marks (ChIP-seq) to predict expression in each cell type.

Manuscripts in preparation

Tokenization

GenomeRL

DNA has no spaces. A reinforcement-learning agent learns where DNA tokens should begin and end, for promoter detection.

Code

Inside transformers

AvalancheBreak

Do periodic separator tokens delay the sudden accuracy collapse of LLMs on long deterministic tasks? A spin-glass analysis on Qwen2.5 and Mistral suggests boundaries act as scaffolding, not new capability.

Code

Disease genetics

Varformer

A set-based transformer for rare-variant disease risk from regulatory and gene-context features, focused on somatic mosaicism in brain disorders.

Manuscript in preparation

Genome editing

EpiCRISPR

Which structural and guide-level features decide the outcome of CRISPR epigenome editing, learned from large guide RNA libraries.

Manuscript in preparation

Earlier, at the University of Tehran’s Robotics and Machine Intelligence Lab, I built a multi-hop co-attention transformer for visual question answering (10% better multimodal reasoning) and human-like game agents learned from 50 players.
